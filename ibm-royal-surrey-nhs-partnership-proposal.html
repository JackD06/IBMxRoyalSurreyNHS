About the Trust

About Royal Surrey NHS Foundation Trust

Royal Surrey NHS Foundation Trust is an award-winning NHS Foundation Trust and teaching hospital based at the Royal Surrey County Hospital in Guildford, serving a population of around 330,000 people across Guildford, Waverley and the wider Surrey region.

The Trust provides a comprehensive range of acute and specialist services — including one of the leading cancer treatment centres in the south of England — and is consistently recognised for high quality care. As a research and education partner with the University of Surrey, Royal Surrey supports clinical innovation, cancer research and the training of the next generation of healthcare professionals.

Royal Surrey's commitment to digital transformation and data-driven care is embedded in its strategy — with AI, digital infrastructure and operational intelligence identified as core enablers of outstanding patient outcomes.

Royal Surrey Strategy  ·  Delivering Excellent Care

Royal Surrey's Strategic Priorities

Royal Surrey's strategy is centred on delivering excellent care for every patient, every time — underpinned by its commitment to people, innovation and sustainability. These priorities represent the areas where intelligent technology creates the most meaningful impact.

Priority 01
Outstanding Patient Care
Delivering safe, effective and compassionate care to every patient — reducing waiting times, improving outcomes and ensuring everyone receives the right care at the right time.
Priority 02
Our People & Culture
Building a Trust where every member of staff feels valued and able to thrive — reducing admin burden, investing in wellbeing and creating a culture of inclusion and excellence.
Priority 03
Research & Innovation
Leveraging the University of Surrey partnership and cancer research capabilities to lead clinical innovation — from AI-assisted diagnostics to personalised medicine and trial design.
Priority 04
Digital Transformation
Harnessing digital and data capabilities to improve the care experience, streamline clinical workflows and unlock the value of the Trust's clinical data for patients and researchers.
Priority 05
Operational & Financial Sustainability
Delivering financial plans by making the best use of resources, reducing waste and improving productivity — ensuring Royal Surrey can invest in the services that matter most.
Priority 06
Sustainability & Net Zero
Delivering the NHS net zero commitment — reducing Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions across the hospital estate, supply chain and procurement for the communities Royal Surrey serves.
